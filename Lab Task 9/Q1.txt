a) What is the probability of drawing a red card (hearts or diamonds)?
=> Total Cards: 52 | Total Red Cards: 26
    P(red) = 26/52 = 1/2

b) Given that you have drawn a red card, what is the probability that the card is a heart?
=> Total Red Cards: 26 | Heart Cards: 13
    P(heart | red) = 13/26 = 1/2

c) Given that the drawn card is a face card (Jack, Queen, King), what is the probability that it is a diamond?
=> Total Face Cards: 12 | Diamond Face Cards: 3
    P(diamond | face) = 3/12 = 1/4

d) Given that the card drawn is a face card, calculate the probability that it is also a spade or a queen.
=> Spade Cards: 3 | Queen Cards: 4 | Queen of Spades: 1 | Total Face Cards: 12
    Total Spade or Queen cards = 3+4-1 = 6
    P(spade, queen | face) = 6/12 = 1/2
